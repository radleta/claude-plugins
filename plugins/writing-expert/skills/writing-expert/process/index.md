# Writing Process

Knowledge hub covering the ordered passes a piece moves through from raw research to final prose: reporting (gather more than you'll use), outlining the spine, rough drafting without self-editing, structural revision before sentence revision, line editing, and writing the opening and title last when you know what the piece actually says.

## Pages

- [multi-pass-sequence](multi-pass-sequence.md) — The ordered passes (reporting → spine → rough draft → structural revision → line edit → opening+title last) and why collapsing them is the most common process failure.
- [reporting-surplus](reporting-surplus.md) — Gather 3–4× more material than you will use; writing from surplus gives selection power, writing from scarcity forces padding.
- [spine-before-draft](spine-before-draft.md) — Write the structure as a separate document before drafting prose; the spine is a distinct writing pass, not mental prep.
- [shitty-first-draft](shitty-first-draft.md) — The first draft is for the writer only: inner critic off, forward motion only, 2–4× target length is normal.
