## Foundational Principles

- **Floor vs. ceiling, complementary not redundant.** human-voice removes what is bad (AI tells, puffery, hollow symmetry, fake warmth) — that is the floor. writing-expert adds what is great (earned tension, precise diction, structural payoff, a real "so what") — that is the ceiling. A piece needs both passes. Never conflate them.
- **Canon-derived, not asserted.** Every pattern in this wiki traces back to exemplars — deconstructed pieces where the technique can be pointed to, not just described. Assertions without exemplars belong in scratch, not here.
- **Operational, not academic.** Every knowledge page must end in a `## How to apply` section. Craft knowledge that cannot be translated into a concrete action on a draft has no home here.

## Scope Boundaries

- **De-slop / humanizing** → `human-voice`. That skill strips what should not be there. This wiki teaches what should be added. Invoke both, in that order, on any piece that goes out under a person's name.
