# Narrative Craft

Knowledge hub covering the structural moves that make a piece hold together: how a strong opening earns the reader's attention, how to find and maintain the throughline, how to build and release tension, and how to land a "so what" that makes the piece feel necessary rather than optional.

## Pages

- [openings](openings.md) — The opening as a contract with the reader: sets register, stakes, and voice while creating a question the piece will answer.
- [throughline](throughline.md) — The single connective thread the reader follows across the whole piece; every section must advance, complicate, or resolve it.
- [structural-tension](structural-tension.md) — Creating and sustaining the gap between what the reader knows and what they need to know; includes scene vs. summary discipline.
- [so-what](so-what.md) — Landing the earned meaning at the end: not a summary, but the answer to why the reader just spent their time.
