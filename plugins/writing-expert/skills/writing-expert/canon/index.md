# Canon

Knowledge hub covering the corpus of exemplary technical and long-form writing pieces — deconstructed to extract the specific craft moves that make them work. Each entry names the exemplar, identifies the technique, and translates it into a reusable pattern applicable to new pieces.

The defining constraint: every pattern here traces back to at least two named exemplars. Patterns asserted without pointing to specific pieces belong in scratch, not here.

## Pages

- [The Lens Essay Pattern](lens-essay-pattern.md) — Use an unexpected comparator domain as a consistent interpretive lens; mined from Paul Graham's "Hackers and Painters" and Orwell's "Politics and the English Language."
- [The Problem-Solution-Paradox-Resolution Structure](problem-solution-paradox.md) — Four-beat structure that keeps readers engaged past the reveal; mined from Gawande's "The Checklist" and Gladwell's long-form essays.
- [The Recursive-Surprise Pattern](recursive-surprise.md) — Individually ordinary steps that compound into an extraordinary conclusion; mined from Ken Thompson's Turing lecture and McPhee's structural theory.
- [The Historical-Scaffolding Argument](historical-scaffolding.md) — Build an abstract claim from compounding specific episodes; mined from Coates's "The Case for Reparations" and Taubes's investigative journalism.
