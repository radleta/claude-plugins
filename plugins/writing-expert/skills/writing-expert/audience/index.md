# Audience

Knowledge hub covering how to calibrate writing for specific technical communities: what the technical reader expects and resents, how Hacker News and Lobsters surface and reward posts (and how they punish ones that oversell), and which credibility signals earn trust vs. which backfire.

## Pages

- [reader-expectation-framework](reader-expectation-framework.md) — Gopen & Swan's topic-stress position model: readers assign weight by sentence position, not just content. Topic position = context; stress position = new/important info.
- [hn-lobsters-reader-profile](hn-lobsters-reader-profile.md) — What HN/Lobsters technical readers bring: high prior knowledge, evaluation mode, hype pattern-recognition, and an early-exit threshold that fires if depth is absent in the first two paragraphs.
- [credibility-signals](credibility-signals.md) — Decision table: what earns trust (specific numbers, named failure modes, honest tradeoffs) vs. what triggers skepticism (superlatives, all-positive framing, product-page vocabulary).
